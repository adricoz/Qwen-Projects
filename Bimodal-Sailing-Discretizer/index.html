Operating profile editor

Where the boat sails, as a few modes. Drag the shapes on the map; set each mode's weight here. Each peak has a broad, light "tail" mode: together they make a smooth valley between the peaks. The export lists the VPP conditions computed here, ready for vpp_acceleration.

Hand-placed conditions

Shift+click on the map to add one, then drag it. Its weight is a share of the total probability, taken from the modes.

The hand-placed weights reach 100 %: nothing is left for the modes. Export is disabled.
